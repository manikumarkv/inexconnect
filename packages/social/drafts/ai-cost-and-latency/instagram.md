## Carousel slides

Design in the brand template (coral #FF5C50, one idea per slide, big type).

- Slide 1 (cover): AI cost and latency
- Slide 2: Cost is tokens in times the input price plus tokens out times the output price. Output tokens cost five times more than input on current Claude models.
- Slide 3: Latency comes mostly from model size and how many tokens the model must read and write. Time to first token and total time are different numbers.
- Slide 4: The biggest levers are the smallest model that passes your evals, shorter prompts and answers, prompt caching and the Batch API.
- Slide 5: Streaming doesn't make a request cheaper or finish sooner, but users see the answer start much earlier.
