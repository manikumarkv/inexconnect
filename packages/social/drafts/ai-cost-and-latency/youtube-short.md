**Title:** AI cost and latency in 60 seconds #Shorts

## Script (~104 words — aim for 130–150)

Record as screen capture + face cam or voiceover. Put each point on screen as big text.

- [0–3s · HOOK] AI cost and latency: what drives them and how to cut them
- [3–18s · POINT 1] Cost is tokens in times the input price plus tokens out times the output price. Output tokens cost five times more than input on current Claude models.
- [18–33s · POINT 2] Latency comes mostly from model size and how many tokens the model must read and write. Time to first token and total time are different numbers.
- [33–48s · POINT 3] The biggest levers are the smallest model that passes your evals, shorter prompts and answers, prompt caching and the Batch API.
- [~50s · CTA] The full guide is on our blog — link in the description. Follow for more AI dev tips.
