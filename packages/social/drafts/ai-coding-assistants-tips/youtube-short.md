**Title:** Getting the most out of AI coding assistants in 60 seconds #Shorts

## Script (~98 words — aim for 130–150)

Record as screen capture + face cam or voiceover. Put each point on screen as big text.

- [0–3s · HOOK] Getting the most out of AI coding assistants
- [3–18s · POINT 1] An AI coding assistant is only as good as the context you give it, so write your project's rules down once in a file like CLAUDE.md or AGENTS.md.
- [18–33s · POINT 2] Ask for one small, clear task at a time, with the goal, the limits and the files to look at.
- [33–48s · POINT 3] Give the assistant a check it can run, such as your tests, and ask it to show the result instead of saying it's done.
- [~50s · CTA] The full guide is on our blog — link in the description. Follow for more AI dev tips.

## Description

Practical habits for AI coding assistants: give project context, ask for small tasks, make the AI test its work, and review every line it writes.
