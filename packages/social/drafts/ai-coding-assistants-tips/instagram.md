## Carousel slides

Design in the brand template (coral #FF5C50, one idea per slide, big type).

- Slide 1 (cover): Getting the most out of AI coding assistants
- Slide 2: An AI coding assistant is only as good as the context you give it, so write your project's rules down once in a file like CLAUDE.md or AGENTS.md.
- Slide 3: Ask for one small, clear task at a time, with the goal, the limits and the files to look at.
- Slide 4: Give the assistant a check it can run, such as your tests, and ask it to show the result instead of saying it's done.
- Slide 5: Read every line of AI-written code before you merge it, and never paste secrets into a prompt.
