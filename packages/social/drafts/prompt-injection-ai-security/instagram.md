## Carousel slides

Design in the brand template (coral #FF5C50, one idea per slide, big type).

- Slide 1 (cover): Prompt injection and AI security
- Slide 2: Prompt injection is text that tries to give your AI new instructions. It can come from the user (direct) or from content the AI reads, like a web page or an email (indirect).
- Slide 3: A model reads instructions and data as the same kind of text, so it can't reliably tell them apart. There is no known way to prevent injection completely.
- Slide 4: The damage depends on what the AI can do. Tools, agents and MCP servers that act on your systems raise the stakes.
- Slide 5: Defend in layers, in your code, not only in the prompt. Least privilege, human approval for risky actions, labelled untrusted input, output checks, allow-lists, logs and evals.
