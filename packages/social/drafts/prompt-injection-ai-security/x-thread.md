### Post 1 (65/280)

Prompt injection and AI security: what developers need to know 🧵👇

### Post 2 (177/280)

1/ Prompt injection is text that tries to give your AI new instructions. It can come from the user (direct) or from content the AI reads, like a web page or an email (indirect).

### Post 3 (157/280)

2/ A model reads instructions and data as the same kind of text, so it can't reliably tell them apart. There is no known way to prevent injection completely.

### Post 4 (117/280)

3/ The damage depends on what the AI can do. Tools, agents and MCP servers that act on your systems raise the stakes.

### Post 5 (179/280)

4/ Defend in layers, in your code, not only in the prompt. Least privilege, human approval for risky actions, labelled untrusted input, output checks, allow-lists, logs and evals.
