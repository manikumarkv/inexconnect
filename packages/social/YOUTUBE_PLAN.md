# YouTube plan

**Goal:** YouTube is the main marketing channel. Teach developers and
business owners practical AI, so they trust iNexConnect to build for them.
Every video ends with the blog link and the free discovery call
(`inexconnect.com/book`).

## Channel positioning

- **Name / handle:** iNexConnect · `@inexconnect`
- **Promise:** "Practical AI for building real software — in plain English."
- **Banner text:** Idea • Build • Grow — AI-powered apps & web development
- **Trailer (60s):** who we are, what the channel teaches, one quick demo.

## Content series

| Series                        | Format                           | Source posts                               | Cadence                          |
| ----------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------- |
| **AI in 60 seconds**          | Short                            | "What is" posts (MCP, RAG, LLM, agents…)   | 2 / week                         |
| **Build with me**             | Long, 10–15 min screen recording | "Build your first…" How-to posts           | 1 every 2 weeks                  |
| **Claude certification prep** | Long playlist, 8–12 min each     | the 5 certification-prep series (30 posts) | after "Build with me" catches up |
| **Our builds**                | Long demo, 5–8 min               | DevRunway, TrueIcon MCP, ImageRight MCP    | 1 / month                        |
| **Client stories**            | Short + long                     | real projects (with permission)            | as they happen                   |

Start with Shorts — they are fast to make and grow subscribers. Long videos
are what bring discovery calls.

## Production workflow (per video)

1. **Script** — start from the generated `youtube-short.md` / `youtube-long.md`.
2. **Record** — screen capture (OBS or Loom) + mic; face cam optional.
   Shorts: vertical 1080×1920. Long: 1920×1080.
3. **Edit** — CapCut or DaVinci Resolve. Add captions (most people watch muted).
4. **Thumbnail** — brand coral `#FF5C50`, ≤4 big words, one face or screenshot.
5. **Upload** — title, description and chapters from the draft. Add to the
   series playlist. Schedule in YouTube Studio for the calendar date.
6. **Repurpose** — cut 2–3 Shorts from each long video; share the long video
   on LinkedIn the same day.

## Upload checklist

- [ ] Title has the main keyword in the first 40 characters
- [ ] Description: first 2 lines sell the video; blog link + `/book` link
- [ ] Chapters (first one at 00:00)
- [ ] Captions checked
- [ ] Custom thumbnail
- [ ] Added to a playlist
- [ ] End screen → next video in the series + subscribe
- [ ] Pinned comment with a question
